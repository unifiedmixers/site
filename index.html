UNIFIED MIXERS

the workgroup model gateway

One binary that owns model traffic on machines you control. It holds your servers — vLLM, llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio, OpenRouter — and hands every client (Copilot BYOK, opencode, codex, DSH, curl) one clean API. System prompts are rewritten server-side, broken tool calls repaired, flaky exchanges retried, every byte captured, the ledger kept here instead of in every client.

PRE-M1 · BUILDING IN PUBLIC

What exists nowhere else

  1. Declarative per-model system-prompt injection for arbitrary OpenAI clients — personalities as rule files, applied upstream, invisible to the client.
  2. Named per-model parameter presets surfaced in the catalog — qwen3.8:Think, the variant grammar OpenRouter clients already speak.
  3. First-class local capture of upstream bytes, before and after rewriting, with replay. No external observability stack.
  4. The single-owner, loopback form factor — one binary, no Postgres, no Redis, no user table. Workgroups share it through hand-minted tokens with caps and attribution.
